CRISOT batch outputs were merged into a guide-level summary and then integrated with the FOTR guide table. The CRISOT batch results contained 105 unique spacer targets and 4,321 total mutation-level rows. The main FOTR table contained 145 guide rows but 105 unique spacers, because some spacers appear with different PAM/context records. Exact 23-mer matching was not appropriate because the CRISOT outputs used TGG as the PAM for all records, whereas the FOTR table includes CGG, TGG, GGG, and AGG PAMs. Therefore, CRISOT was merged at spacer level. This yielded CRISOT coverage for all 145 FOTR guide rows. In downstream interpretation, the CRISOT comparison should be described as spacer-level external comparison, not exact PAM-specific validation.
