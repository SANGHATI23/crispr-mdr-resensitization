FOTR-prioritized guide ranking was applied to 145 candidate guides after integration of activity, specificity, conservation, RNA accessibility, binding-risk, and RNA-structure-risk features. In this first implementation, functional severity and genomic context penalty were retained as neutral placeholders pending downstream annotation, while RNA structural accessibility and off-target hit burden were incorporated directly from the available guide-level features. FOTR prioritization changed the top-ranked guide for three of four resistance genes. For blaKPC, the top FOTR guide was TTGTCATCCTTGTTAGGCGG, an Accessible guide with a FOTR priority score of 91.10. For blaNDM1, FOTR replaced the original top guide, which was Structurally_Risky, with the Accessible guide ACGCCCGCATACCGAACCAT, which achieved a FOTR priority score of 93.70. For mcr1, FOTR selected ACCATCCAGTACCAGAAGAA, an Accessible guide with no predicted spacer pairing and a FOTR priority score of 97.36. For mecA, the original top guide ACTGAACGTCCGATAAAGGT remained the FOTR top candidate, indicating that the highest-scoring mecA guide was also structurally accessible. These results show that RNA- and risk-aware prioritization can alter candidate selection beyond the original activity/conservation score alone.
