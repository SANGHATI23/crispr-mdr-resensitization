Reviewer-safe interpretation:
This large-scale external experiment applies the same weak-supervision candidate-ranking logic to DeepHF CRISPR guide-activity datasets containing more than 55,000 guides per condition. It addresses the scale/generalization concern of the 90-guide AMR analysis by showing that the evaluation protocol can be applied to substantially larger public CRISPR guide datasets. It does not experimentally validate the AMR guide rankings.
