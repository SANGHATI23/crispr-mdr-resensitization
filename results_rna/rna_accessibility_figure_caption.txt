Figure X. RNA structural accessibility classification of candidate CRISPR guide RNAs across four antimicrobial resistance genes. Full sgRNA sequences were evaluated using RNAfold, including the 20-nt spacer and SpCas9 sgRNA scaffold. Guides were classified as Accessible, Moderate, or Structurally_Risky based on seed-region pairing and overall spacer pairing. The distribution shows that RNA structural filtering substantially affects candidate prioritization, especially for mecA, where most candidate guides were classified as structurally risky.
