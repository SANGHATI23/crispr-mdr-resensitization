RNA structural accessibility analysis was performed on 145 full sgRNA sequences consisting of the 20-nt spacer followed by the SpCas9 sgRNA scaffold. Overall, 75 guides (51.72%) were classified as Structurally_Risky, 50 guides (34.48%) as Moderate, and 20 guides (13.79%) as Accessible. The distribution varied substantially by resistance gene. blaKPC contained 6 Accessible guides (17.65%), 14 Moderate guides (41.18%), and 14 Structurally_Risky guides (41.18%). blaNDM1 contained 9 Accessible guides (19.57%), 20 Moderate guides (43.48%), and 17 Structurally_Risky guides (36.96%). mcr1 showed a higher structural-risk burden, with 24 of 42 guides (57.14%) classified as Structurally_Risky. The strongest structural filtering effect was observed for mecA, where 20 of 23 guides (86.96%) were Structurally_Risky and only 1 guide (4.35%) was Accessible. These results indicate that RNA structural accessibility can substantially alter guide prioritization, particularly for genes such as mecA where most otherwise valid candidate guides show predicted spacer or seed-region occlusion.
